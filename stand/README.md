# Desk stand (v3.3)

Printed desk stand for the CrowPanel 2.1" round display. The display mounts in a threaded cup that a nut clamps through the front face of the base. A back plate closes the rear opening flush with the rim.

| File | Part | Print orientation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `base.stl` | Base: serrated cup seat, internal stop ledge, snap pockets, buzzer rails | Front face down |
| `cup.stl` | Threaded cup that holds the display, serrated to lock its rotation | As exported |
| `nut.stl` | Nut that clamps the cup to the base | As exported |
| `back_plate.stl` | Flush snap-in back plate with grille and USB cable notch | Outer face down (tabs up) |

## Assembly

1. Push the cup through the front of the base, with the teeth lined up at the angle you want, and screw the nut on from inside.
2. Slide the KY-006 buzzer into the rails on the bottom wall, near the back opening.
3. Lay the USB cable into the notch at the bottom of the back plate, then press the plate in until all four tabs click. Its face should sit flush with the rim.

## Notes

- The back plate fits only this base. Earlier bases have no ledge or pockets.
- If the snap pockets show through the side walls, raise the slicer's wall loop count to about 5.
