What the buzzer can do
  • One tone at a time. No chords; very fast note runs fake one.
  • Instant on and off. No real fades; thinning pulses fake one.
  • No true noise, but fast random pitches get close (see Explosion, Static).
  • Volume is a device setting, not per sound. Sounds marked soft play one step quieter.
  • Best from 1 to 5 kHz, loudest near 2.3 kHz. Below 450 Hz it's thin; above 6 kHz it gets piercing.
  • This computer's speakers color the sound. Connect the desk buddy to hear the real thing.

Save and share
