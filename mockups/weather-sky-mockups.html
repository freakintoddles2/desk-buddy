Living sky with weather

Mock-up for Living sky following the current weather. One painter covers every state: each Open-Meteo weather code sets a few numbers (cloud cover, cloud darkness, sky gray, rain or snow amount, fog, lightning), so there's no image per state. Pixels are quantized to RGB565 with the device's Bayer dither. Clouds drift a little each minute. Click a sky to see it full size.

Open-Meteo also returns cloud cover as a percent (cloud_cover), which would give smooth steps between the code's four cloud levels.

Rain styles

The same rain at different strengths, drawn with each combination of the ideas: (1) bolder streaks, (2) splashes along the bottom, (3) drips running down the glass, (4) rain curtains under the clouds, (5) a cooler, slate-blue cast. Click any sky to see it full size. The rows below the comparison use the style picked in the builder, which starts on the chosen style: 1 + 2 + 3 + 5.

How it would run on the device

PieceApproach
CloudsA 64 x 64 byte noise table (4 KB, built once at boot), sampled at two scales with bilinear lookups. The cover level sets a threshold, darkness sets the color. Nothing stored per state.
Rain, drizzle, sleetSlanted streaks from an integer hash of the column: no stored drops, a few adds and a compare per pixel.
Snow, grains, hailOne hashed dot per grid cell, two layers of cell sizes. Cells that miss the density test cost one compare.
FogA blend toward the fog color, stronger toward the bottom, with soft bands from the same noise table.
LightningOne fixed zigzag bolt with a fork (14 segments) to the right of the clock, with a bright patch in the clouds behind it (larger and violet at night). Only pixels near its bounding box check the segments.
Sun and moonWhen clouds cover them the painter draws them under the clouds and Home hides the sprite (like Heat does). In clear weather the sprite stays.
LegibilityRain and snow fade to about a third inside an oval around the clock and date.
RepaintAs now, once a minute, plus once when new weather arrives. Which weather: the current conditions; if those are over 3 hours old, the forecast for this hour (the forecast covers 30 hours); with no weather data at all (no Wi-Fi, no location, never fetched), Mainly clear.

Measured on the device (2026-11-26, core 0, 480 px): ~0.3 s clear, ~0.6-0.8 s cloudy or foggy, ~1.2-1.6 s rain or snow, ~2 s a thunderstorm. Clouds are sampled every 2 px and interpolated, and each column's or cell's random numbers are cached, which took rain from ~2.6 s to ~1.2 s. Home keeps the last painted sky up while the next one paints.
