Tapping a row that isn't in the middle

Each device has the same list. Tap a row above or below the outline and watch what moves. Switch between the Panels list (tap flips a switch) and the Settings list (tap opens a screen). Turn up / Turn down act as the knob.

Now

  • Panels: the switch flips and the list scrolls the tapped row to the middle.
  • Settings: the first tap only scrolls it to the middle; a second tap opens it.

A: tap acts in place, nothing moves (recommended)

  • The tapped row just does its thing: flips, or opens, on the first tap. The list stays put and the row flashes.
  • The outline stays where the knob left it: it is the knob's place, not the finger's.
  • Simplest rule for every list: drag to scroll, tap to act, turn to move the outline.

B: tap acts in place, the outline follows the finger

  • Same as A, and the outline jumps to the tapped row without scrolling.
  • The next knob turn carries on from the tapped row (the list scrolls then).
  • The outline sits off-centre until you turn, unlike the rest of the lists.
