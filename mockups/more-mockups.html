Showing that a list goes on

The Settings list (13 rows). Drag on a screen to scroll it, or use the buttons. Look at the bottom of each screen at the top of the list, then scroll to the end.

Now

  • Rows end cleanly above "1 / 13"; nothing looks cut off, so the list reads as complete.

A: a half row peeks at the bottom

  • The list window ends mid-row, so the next row is visibly cut in half under a short fade: the usual phone cue.
  • The counter moves below the cut; at the end of the list no row peeks.
  • Costs nothing on screen; works for every list the same way.

B: curved scroll bar on the right edge

  • A thin arc follows the round edge (as round watches do): its length is how much shows, its place is where you are.
  • Replaces the "1 / 13" counter. Always visible, or only while scrolling.

C: A + B together (recommended)

  • The peeking row says "there's more right here"; the arc says how much and where you are.
  • The counter goes; the arc carries its job.
