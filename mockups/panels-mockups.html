Panels screen: show, hide and reorder

All three options drop the distance fading: every row is the same size and brightness, and only the selected row has an outline. Use the buttons under each device as the knob (Turn up, Turn down, Press), or tap rows directly. Home and Settings can't be hidden.

Now (for reference)

  • Rows fade and shrink as they move away from the selection.
  • Hiding a panel: press, then Hide on a second screen.

A: switches, plus a Reorder mode

  • Press (or tap a row) flips its switch: one step to show or hide.
  • The last row is Reorder: rows get grips, press picks one up, turn to move, press to drop. Hold or Done leaves.
  • Clear and familiar (phone settings). Moving is a separate mode, two presses away.

A2: switches, Reorder mode with arrows

  • The list is the same as A: press or tap flips the switch, and the last row is Reorder….
  • In Reorder, each row gets ▲ and ▼. Tap one to move the row one place; the ends grey out.
  • The knob still works there: press to pick a row up, turn to move it, press to drop it (the arrows are too small to aim at with the knob).
  • Hold or Done leaves Reorder.

B: switches, plus press-and-turn to move

  • Press flips the switch, as in A.
  • Moving: hold the knob down and turn it (the row lifts), or drag a row by its grip with a finger. No mode at all.
  • Fastest, but press-and-turn is new on this device and nothing on screen teaches it except the foot hint. In the mock-up, Hold + turn stands in for it.

C: eye icon, press to pick up

  • Tap the eye to show or hide; tap the name (or press the knob) to pick the row up and move it, press again to drop.
  • The knob alone can't hide without a second target, so the knob's press moves, and the eye is touch-only. Knob users would turn to the eye with an extra step (shown: turning while a row is selected moves between name and eye).
  • Most compact; splits roles between touch and knob, which the rest of the device avoids.
