Panel nav: current vs. two options

Each device shows the same panel. Pick a panel under each device, or use the left and right arrow keys to swipe all three. The fast travel tray (pull down from the top edge) is the same in every option, so it isn't drawn.

Now: pill with the panel name

For reference. On list panels (Settings, Habits, Days, Play) the pill also works as the screen's title.

B: icons only

  • Same strip, no word. The current icon is larger and white with a blue glow; neighbours stay faint.
  • Keeps the "where am I, what's next door" hint and the running dots (yellow = a timer is going).
  • List panels lose their written title; the icon has to carry it.

C: gone

  • Cleanest; the strip's band at the top is free (Home's sun no longer collides with the strip at midday).
  • Lists have no title at all, and the running dots go away (the Home glance row still shows a running focus or timer).
  • Nothing hints that more panels exist; new people find them by swiping or by the tray.
