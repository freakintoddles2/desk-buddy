Customize Home

Swipe up from the bottom edge of Home (drag with the mouse, or press U). Choose which elements show (Weather, Habits, Countdown, Timers & focus; the clock and date always stay) and the face. Knob: scroll wheel or ↑ ↓ to turn, Enter to press, hold Enter or Escape to close. The faces are simplified versions of the earlier Home mock-ups. Option B (edit in place) was dropped.
