Going back from a sub-screen

Swipe and knob-hold keep working in every option; these add something you can see. Switch the screen under each device; tap the back control to try it (it flashes "Back to Settings"). Option B replays its hint with Show hint.

Now

  • Nothing on screen says how to leave; only swipe or hold.

A: chevron in the title (recommended)

  • "‹ PANELS": the title is the back button, as on phones. No extra space.
  • Slightly brighter than a plain title so it reads as tappable; lights up on touch.
  • One change in the shared title helper covers every sub-screen.

A2: round back button beside the title

  • A separate 32 px button left of the title: a bigger, clearer target than the title text.
  • Takes room on the round screen's top edge; long titles push it outward toward the curve.

B: hint the first few times

  • "Swipe or hold to go back", plus a glow that slides in from the left edge, for ~2.5 s.
  • Shown the first 3 times any sub-screen opens, then never again (a counter in flash).
  • Teaches the gestures (knob too); nothing permanent on screen. Can be combined with A.

C: Back chip at the bottom

  • Most obvious, and the knob can reach it.
  • Costs the bottom row; screens that already have Done get two exits (Brightness keeps only Done).
